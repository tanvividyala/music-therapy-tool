# Muiso
Streaming recommendations are shaped by sponsorships and other commercial factors, ignore the listener's mental state, and rarely check whether a song's lyrics or tempo are calming. The Therapeutic Music Recommender takes a user's mood, stress level and goal (such as winding down or focusing) from a short questionnaire and builds a Spotify playlist scored on calming lyrics, tempo (BPM) and genre. Following the iso principle from music therapy (hence our name, Muiso!), the playlist starts where the user is and gradually moves toward their goal, with an explanation for each song. It is meant for students and anyone who uses music to manage stress, anxiety or sleep, and music therapists can use it as a starting point for session playlists.

## Features

### Goal-based sessions
Choose a goal: Calm, Focus, Sleep, Uplift, or Release. muiso builds a playlist that starts at your current mood and moves toward that goal, following the structure a music therapist uses for a session.

### Mood check-in
A short questionnaire asks how you feel, how stressed you are, what you want out of the session, and how long you want to listen (30 to 60 minutes). muiso suggests a goal based on your answers, and you can pick a different one.

### Mood-trajectory playlists
Each song gets a position on a valence (positivity) and arousal (energy) map, based on lyric sentiment, BPM, and genre. Songs are ordered along a path from your starting mood to your goal in four stages: Meet, Shift, Core, and Settle.

### Release mode
Release sessions start with heavier songs, often ones with quiet verses and loud choruses, and end on lighter ones.

### Guided listening
You can listen in the browser while muiso shows a note for each song: the current stage, why the song was picked, and a short prompt such as "notice your breathing."

### Built from your taste
muiso uses your top Spotify artists and genres to choose songs and saves the finished playlist to your Spotify library.
