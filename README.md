# Muiso
Streaming recommendations are shaped by sponsorships and other commercial factors, ignore the listener's mental state, and rarely check whether a song's lyrics or tempo are calming. The Therapeutic Music Recommender takes a user's mood, stress level and goal (such as winding down or focusing) from a short questionnaire and builds a Spotify playlist scored on calming lyrics, tempo (BPM) and genre. Following the iso principle from music therapy (hence our name, Mu**iso**!), the playlist starts where the user is and gradually moves toward their goal, with an explanation for each song. It is meant for students and anyone who uses music to manage stress, anxiety or sleep, and music therapists can use it as a starting point for session playlists.
